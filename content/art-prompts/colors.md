# Coat color prompts (Grok Imagine)

Read `STYLE.md` first. Paste **STYLE + the prompt block** for each slug. Save as `public/colors/{slug}.webp`.

---

## bay → `bay.webp`

Educational illustration of a bay horse, side/three-quarter view, calm standing pose. Body coat rich reddish-brown to mahogany. **Black points required:** black mane, black tail, and black lower legs. No white face markings that dominate. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or watermarks. Clear ID of bay for horse education.

---

## chestnut → `chestnut.webp`

Educational illustration of a chestnut (sorrel) horse, side/three-quarter view, calm standing pose. Body coat copper-red to reddish. Mane and tail same red family (not black). Lower legs match body color (not black points). Soft pasture-neutral background. No text. Clear ID of chestnut, distinct from bay.

---

## black → `black.webp`

Educational illustration of a true black horse, side/three-quarter view, calm standing pose. Coat, mane, and tail are solid black without brown burnish in sunlight. No white markings. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text. Clear ID of black coat.

## gray → `gray.webp`

Educational illustration of a gray horse, side/three-quarter view, calm standing pose. Coat is medium gray with visible flecking or dapples (not solid white, not solid black). Darker mane/tail optional but still gray family. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text. Clear ID of gray, not white.

## palomino → `palomino.webp`

Educational illustration of a palomino horse, side/three-quarter view, calm standing pose. Golden / yellow-gold body with **white or flaxen mane and tail** (cream-white, not black). Soft pasture-neutral background. No text. Clear ID of palomino.

## dun → `dun.webp`

Educational illustration of a dun horse, side/three-quarter view, calm standing pose. Dilute tan/gold-dun body. **Required primitive marks:** dark dorsal stripe down the spine and darker points on legs/mane. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text. Clear ID of dun (stripe must be obvious).

## paint → `paint.webp`

Educational illustration of a Paint / pinto patterned horse (tobiano-style preferred), side/three-quarter view, calm standing pose. Large, bold white patches with crisp edges crossing the back, plus colored body patches (bay or chestnut base). Soft pasture-neutral background. No text. Clear ID of paint/pinto spotting—not Appaloosa spots.

## appaloosa → `appaloosa.webp` (shipped as `appaloosa_blanket.jpg`)

Mottled skin cues optional near muzzle/eyes if readable. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text. Clear ID of Appaloosa spotting—not Paint blocks.
