# Distinctive feature prompts (Grok Imagine)

Read `STYLE.md` first. Paste **STYLE + the prompt block** for each slug. Save as `public/features/{slug}.webp`.

For anatomy: same calm horse, soft pasture; make the named landmark visually obvious without labels/arrows (slightly clearer lighting or natural silhouette emphasis).
For markings: same pose; the marking must dominate the ID read.

---

## withers → `withers.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse in calm side/three-quarter standing pose. Emphasize the **withers**—the ridge between the shoulder blades at the base of the neck—so the highest point of the back above the shoulders is clearly readable. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text, arrows, or labels.

---

## poll → `poll.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse head/neck in calm three-quarter view. Emphasize the **poll**—the bony top of the head just behind the ears. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

---

## muzzle → `muzzle.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse head in calm three-quarter view. Emphasize the **muzzle**—soft nose and mouth area, nostrils and lips clearly readable. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

## cannon → `cannon.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse in calm side standing pose. Emphasize a front **cannon bone** region—the long lower leg between knee and fetlock—so that segment is clearly the focus. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

---

## hock → `hock.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse in calm side standing pose. Emphasize a rear **hock** joint—the large angled joint of the hind leg—clearly readable. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

## star → `star.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse head in calm three-quarter view. Clear white **star** marking centered on the forehead (small-to-medium white patch). Rest of face mostly solid color. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

## blaze → `blaze.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse head in calm three-quarter view. Wide white **blaze** running down the face from forehead toward the muzzle. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

## snip → `snip.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse head in calm three-quarter view. Small white **snip** on the muzzle between or just above the nostrils; forehead unmarked or nearly so. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

## sock → `sock.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse in calm side standing pose. One white **sock** marking on a lower leg—white from the hoof up covering the ankle/fetlock area but not reaching the knee/hock. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels.

## stocking → `stocking.webp`

Educational illustration of a horse in calm side standing pose. One white **stocking** marking—white covering the lower leg up to (or near) the knee or hock. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels. Distinct from a short sock.

## piebald → `piebald.webp`

Educational illustration of a **piebald** horse (black-and-white pinto pattern), calm side/three-quarter standing pose. Bold black body patches with large white patches. Soft pasture-neutral background. No text or labels. Clear black-and-white paint pattern, not bay-and-white.
