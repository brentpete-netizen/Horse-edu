# Horse Edu — Grok Imagine style bible

Locked look for coat-color and distinctive-feature educational art. Generate one image per prompt; drop into `public/colors/{slug}.webp` or `public/features/{slug}.webp`.

## Overall
- Educational illustration, not photo-real, not cartoon mashup.
- Clear side view or gentle three-quarter view of a single adult horse.
- Same calm standing pose across the set (weight even, head slightly forward, four feet on ground).
- Soft pasture-neutral background: muted green/tan field, soft sky, no fences clutter, no barns, no people.
- No text, logos, watermarks, arrows, or labels in the image (UI labels the page).
- Square or landscape framing with the horse filling most of the frame; leave a little breathing room at edges.
- Soft even lighting; avoid heavy drama, neon, or painterly brush noise that hides coat cues.

## Identification first
- Coat color and markings must read at a glance from across a room.
- Points, stripes, white patterns, and anatomy landmarks must be anatomically plausible.
- Prefer one clean example over “interesting” variants that confuse ID.

## Consistency
- Same breed type feel (generic stock/warmblood outline) unless a prompt says otherwise.
- Same mane/tail length style across the color set.
- Export as WebP (preferred) or PNG; filename must match the slug exactly.

## Do not
- Add multiple horses, foals, or riders.
- Add UI chrome, captions, or “Bay horse” lettering.
- Use Wikipedia-style collage layouts or cutouts on white void (use soft pasture).
