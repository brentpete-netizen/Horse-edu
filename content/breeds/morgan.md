## Overview

The Morgan is America’s earliest foundation breed, known for stamina, vigor, a strong natural way of moving, and a kind disposition. Compact and versatile, Morgans compete and work in driving, English and Western disciplines, dressage, endurance, and therapeutic riding.

## Key characteristics

Typical height 14.1–15.2 hands. Expressive head with broad forehead and large eyes; arched neck from a well-angulated shoulder; compact body with short back, close coupling, deep heart girth; high-set tail. Straight, sound legs with short cannons. Distinctive for stamina, personality, and strong natural movement. Many colors including bay, black, chestnut/brown, and cream dilutes (brown is common but not a separate Notion color option).

## History

Traces to Figure (Justin Morgan’s horse), foaled 1789. Mid-1800s breeders in Vermont and western New Hampshire concentrated Morgan lines; Morgans set early trotting records and served as cavalry mounts (e.g. First Vermont Cavalry in the Civil War). Influential in forming Standardbred, Quarter Horse, Tennessee Walking Horse, and American Saddlebred. American Morgan Horse Association (AMHA) maintains the registry.
