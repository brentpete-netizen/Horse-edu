## Overview

The Thoroughbred is the premier racing breed, known for speed, stamina, and athletic ability over longer distances.

## Key characteristics

Tall, refined build with long legs, deep chest, and elegant head. Exceptional speed and endurance.

## History

Developed in 17th–18th century England from three foundation stallions (Byerley Turk, Darley Arabian, Godolphin Arabian) crossed with native mares.
