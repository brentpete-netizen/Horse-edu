## Overview

The American Paint Horse combines the stock horse abilities of the Quarter Horse with distinctive pinto color patterns.

## Key characteristics

Tobiano or overo color patterns over a stock-horse build. Versatile and athletic.

## History

Developed from Quarter Horse and Thoroughbred stock with color patterns; APHA registry emphasizes both color and performance.
