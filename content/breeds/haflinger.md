## Overview

The Haflinger is a chestnut all-round pleasure breed from the Tyrolean mountains, known for a pale/flaxen mane and tail, willing temperament, and versatile athleticism. The American Haflinger Registry preserves pedigrees and publishes breeding objectives used for inspection and shows.

## Key characteristics

Desired height 54–60 in (~13.2–15.0 hh); below 54 in strongly discouraged for breeding. Colour pale chestnut to dark liver chestnut with pale/white/flaxen mane and tail (not a separate Palomino base colour). Colour impurities and excessive leg white discouraged. Elegant, harmonious type with lean expressive head, large eyes, well-formed neck, correct defined limbs; suited as an all-round pleasure horse. Pure breeding after foundation stallion Folie.

## History

Founded on indigenous Tyrolean mountain horses crossed with oriental half-blood El’Bedavi XXII (1868) via stallion Folie (249). After Folie, outcrossing is not permitted under AHR breeding objectives. American Haflinger Registry maintains North American pedigrees and a voluntary Inspection and Classification Program.
