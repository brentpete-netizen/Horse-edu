## Overview

The Friesian is a Dutch baroque-type breed (Friesland; Notion Origin = Other) known for jet-black colour, abundant mane/tail/feather, an impressive front, and elevated movement. The KFPS runs a closed studbook breeding programme for a functional sport and utility horse with a reliable, trainable character.

## Key characteristics

Desired wither height around 165 cm (~16.1 hh); stallion selection minima about 158 cm (young) / 160 cm (4+). Small expressive noble head; long slightly arched vertically set neck; abundant mane, tail, and leg hair (socks); ideally solid black without markings; harmonious, athletic, roughly square upward build; long sloping shoulder; strong back and loin. Character goals emphasize reliability and trainability.

## History

Native to Friesland (Netherlands). KFPS maintains the breed’s origin studbook and closed breeding programme: main-section registration requires both parents in the main section. Breeding goal (Member Council 2021): functionally built utility horse with Friesian breed characteristics, health, sport aptitude, reliable character, and trainability.
