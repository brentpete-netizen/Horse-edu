## Overview

The American Quarter Horse is the most popular breed in the United States, known for its speed over short distances, cow sense, and versatility.

## Key characteristics

Compact, muscular build with a short head, strong hindquarters, and powerful acceleration. Excellent for ranch work and western disciplines.

## History

Developed in colonial America from English Thoroughbreds crossed with Spanish horses; refined for quarter-mile racing and cattle work. AQHA founded in 1940.
