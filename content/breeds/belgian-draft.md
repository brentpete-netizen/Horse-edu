## Overview

The Belgian is a powerful draft breed known for its strength, size, and calm disposition.

## Key characteristics

Massive, muscular build with a short neck, broad chest, and strong legs. Often chestnut with flaxen mane and tail.

## History

Originated in Belgium (Brabant region); popular in the U.S. for farm and logging work. American Belgian is a refined version.
