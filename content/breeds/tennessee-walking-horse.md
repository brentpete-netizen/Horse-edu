## Overview

The Tennessee Walking Horse is a light horse breed founded in Middle Tennessee from Narragansett and Canadian Pacer, Standardbred, Thoroughbred, Morgan, and American Saddlebred stock. It is known for a calm disposition and naturally smooth gaits—especially the flat walk, running walk, and canter—making it popular for show, trail, and pleasure riding.

## Key characteristics

Typically 14.3–17 hands and about 900–1,200 lb. Defined head with small, well-placed ears; long sloping shoulder and hip; fairly short back with short, strong coupling; longer underline than topline for a long stride. Performs the flat foot walk, running walk (an inherited natural gait unique to the breed), and canter; some also show other smooth trail gaits. Comes in many colors and patterns.

## History

Founded in Middle Tennessee as a utility horse from Narragansett and Canadian Pacer, Standardbred, Thoroughbred, Morgan, and American Saddlebred blood. Developed for a smooth, easy ride; today used widely as a recreational and show mount. Tennessee Walking Horse Breeders’ and Exhibitors’ Association (TWHBEA) is the breed registry.
