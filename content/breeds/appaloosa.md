## Overview

The Appaloosa is known for its distinctive spotted coat patterns and versatility as a stock horse.

## Key characteristics

Spotted or patterned coat (leopard, blanket, snowflake, etc.), mottled skin, striped hooves. Strong and agile.

## History

Associated with the Nez Perce tribe in the Pacific Northwest; registry established in the 20th century to preserve the breed.
