## Overview

The Hanoverian is a German warmblood riding horse (Notion Origin = Other) bred for sport—dressage, jumping, and eventing—as well as leisure and driving. The Hannoveraner Verband keeps the studbook of origin for the breed.

## Key characteristics

Target mature height around a mean of 168 cm (~16.2 hh); horses that are too small or too large are undesirable. Noble, performance-ready sport-horse type with clear outlines and dry features; all colours accepted. Breeding aims for rideability, movement, jumping aptitude, and health suited to competition and recreational use.

## History

Selective Hanoverian breeding from the 16th century; Celle State Stud founded 1735 by King George II. Studbook founded 1888. After WWII refined with Trakehner and Thoroughbred blood into today’s rideable warmblood. Breeders’ association formed 1922 (Verband Hannoverscher Warmblutzüchter). Hannoveraner Verband holds the origin studbook.
