## Overview

One of the oldest horse breeds, the Arabian is renowned for its endurance, beauty, and distinctive dished face.

## Key characteristics

Refined head with dished profile, high tail carriage, and floating movement. Excellent endurance and loyalty.

## History

Developed by Bedouin tribes on the Arabian Peninsula over centuries; used as foundation for many modern breeds including Thoroughbred.
